**Billing policy — version 1, effective 2026-11-01**

- Charges and refunds must be explained from the billing system's records.
- An eligible duplicate is a second settled charge for the same account, subscription, billing period, currency and amount. Keep the earlier settled charge; only the later duplicate may be refunded.
- Automatic refunds are limited to duplicates of $25 or less, settled within the previous seven days, with no existing refund against that charge.
- Other refund requests require human review. Do not promise approval or completion timing for those requests.
